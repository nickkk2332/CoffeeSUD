# CoffeeSUD (CR17): quality tiers for what players craft.  A world turns them on with its own
# copy, world/resources/skills/craftquality.txt, or by taking the # off the lines below.
# One tier a line, the fields between tabs:
#   NAME	CHANCE	VALUE	ARMOR	DAMAGE	USES	LEVEL	EFFECTS
# NAME is the word put in the item's name.  CHANCE is a percent, as a formula: @x1 is the
# crafter's level less the item's, @x2 their proficiency in the skill, @x3 the QUALITY of
# the best workstation helping them (Prop_Workstation); a formula is worked left to right,
# so put brackets round each part.  VALUE, ARMOR, DAMAGE and USES
# multiply the item's value, armour, damage and uses; LEVEL is added to its level; EFFECTS
# are put on it, as Prop_HaveResister(fire 10);Prop_WearAdjuster(dex+1).  The tiers are
# tried from the first; the first that rolls under its chance is the item's, and if none
# does, it is plain.  A data skill with QUALITY off, or a recipe with NOQUALITY=1, rolls none.
#masterwork	(@x2/25)+(@x1/4)+(@x3*3)	1.5	1.1	1.1	1.2	3
#fine	(@x2/6)+@x1+(@x3*5)	1.1	1.05	1.05	1.1	1
#crude	30-(@x2/4)-@x1	0.8	0.9	0.9	0.9	0
