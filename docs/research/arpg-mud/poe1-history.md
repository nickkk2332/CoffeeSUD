# Path of Exile 1: expansion and league mechanics over time

[Dossier](README.md) · [Underlying systems](poe1-systems.md) · [Proposals](proposals.md)

Read this as a history of **introductions**, not a list of everything currently
enabled in every league. A league can disappear while its currency, item bases,
bosses or crafting rewards survive elsewhere. “Historical” below means the
original complete activity should not be assumed available today. The precise
2026 acquisition route of every legacy reward has not been revalidated.

The [league index](https://poedb.tw/us/League) supplies the release sequence.
Linked version notes document the introduction rules; old counts, rewards and
balance values in them are historical. Years group releases without treating
Taiwan/NZ/US date differences as separate launches.

| 2.4 Essence | Imprisoned enemies display a guaranteed crafting reward; releasing them starts the fight | A visible recipe ingredient that doubles as an encounter choice. I06, G06 | Recognizable continuing mechanic, with corruption/reward changes later. [2.4](https://poedb.tw/us/Version_2.4.0) |
| 2.5 Breach | Expanding alternate-realm encounter sustained by fighting; fragments unlock bosses and upgrades | Temporary layered spaces with progress-driven duration. G11 | Major later revisions, including Keepers; do not equate the original circle with all modern Breach content. [2.5](https://poedb.tw/us/Version_2.5.0) |
| 2.6 Legacy | Equip limited-use leaguestones to combine selected old mechanics; reliquary rewards | A world author's activity mixer with compatibility and encounter budgets. G24 | Original leaguestone system historical. [2.6](https://poedb.tw/us/Version_2.6.0) |

Three expansion changes deserve separate attention. **Sacrifice of the Vaal**
added corrupted side areas, soul-powered Vaal skills and irreversible item
corruption; **Forsaken Masters** connected NPC services, missions, reputation,
crafting benches and customizable hideouts; **The Awakening** added Act Four
and socketable passive-tree jewels. **Ascendancy** added trap trials, the
Labyrinth and specialized class trees, while **Atlas of Worlds** turned the
map endgame into a connected progression surface. These suggest A05–A08,
I05/I11, G02/G12 and P04/P10 rather than five unrelated engines.

The campaign and endgame changed independently of leagues. **The Fall of
Oriath** replaced repeated difficulties with ten acts and added selectable
Pantheon powers. **War for the Atlas** introduced competing Shaper/Elder
influence and influenced item pools. **Conquerors of the Atlas** replaced the
previous progression with conquerors, watchstones and new influence crafting.
**Echoes of the Atlas** added Maven witnessing and regional content-specializing
trees. These are successive world-progression designs, not four concurrent
requirements. G02, G15 and P10 should support authored alternatives.

## Systems that a league-only list misses

The major version notes also show repeated changes to melee, bow, spell,
summoner, mine and trigger archetypes; mana reservation; defense and ailment
mitigation; flask automation; crafting access; gem variants; rare-monster
legibility; party interaction; and item filtering. These are covered in the
systems guide. They matter because a content module can become unpleasant when
it assumes a combat tempo or item economy the host world does not have.

Temporary races and events are a separate design family: short race seasons,
Descent/Endless Ledge-style runs, Endless Delve, Mayhem, Delirium Everywhere,
Royale and alternate-ascendancy events such as Legacy of Phrecia are useful
references for isolated presets and leaderboards (P12/P13, G24). This is an
event-family survey, not an audited calendar of every race or community
Gauntlet. See [coverage](coverage.md) before treating any event's exact old
rules as verified.

| Event family | Distinctive rule | Proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Descent](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Descent) / [Endless Ledge](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/The_Endless_Ledge) | A constrained run with progression and supply choices outside ordinary campaign services | G01/G12/P12: isolated run state and an explicit end-of-event migration/void policy |
| [Endless Delve](https://poedb.tw/us/Endless_Delve) | A whole mode centered on one otherwise optional activity | G14/G24: an activity can supply a complete campaign loop |
| [Mayhem](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Mayhem) | Extreme extra encounters under event rules | G24/Q08: publish the altered budget and prohibit accidental application to ordinary worlds |
| [Delirium Everywhere](https://poedb.tw/us/Delirium_Everywhere) | Area-wide risk overlay with event-specific exceptions | G19/G24: explicit precedence between a mode and an individual map |
| [Royale and special event variants](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Event) | Different objectives and competitive rules | G16/P12: isolated match semantics; no assumption that campaign combat is PvP-balanced |
| [Legacy of Phrecia](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3721171) | Alternate ascendancies and an Idol-based replacement for Atlas specialization | A06/G02/G24: progression modules can be replaced independently. [Official specialization announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3720737) |

These event references establish design families. Some are historical and some
have returned in different forms; this table does not certify a current event
schedule or every old loot-transfer rule.

## What is worth carrying forward

The repeated successful pattern is a short local choice that contributes to a
larger project: a beast, memory, architect, syndicate member, fragment, worker,
relic or chart. CoffeeSUD should make **that link** cheap to author. A saved
project ledger, a constrained encounter director and a reliable reward
transaction are more valuable than a different hardcoded manager for each row.

Several historical systems are especially attractive in text: Prophecy's
conditional future, Betrayal's relationships, Heist's preparation and escape,
Delve's light-limited routes, Incursion's changing temple, and Expedition's
ordered choices. Very high-density clearing, repeated looting clicks and
sub-second timed windows need substantial adaptation. The cookbook gives
examples that preserve the decisions without requiring an isometric client.
